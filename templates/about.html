Our Mission

Gamifying Sustainability Education

EcoQuest was developed as a Final Year College Mini Project designed to solve the engagement gap in climate education. By transforming complex environmental science topics into gamified modules, timed mastery challenges, unlockable trophies, and verifiable certificates, we motivate students to adopt real-world eco-friendly habits.

Interactive Learning

Structured lessons covering greenhouse emissions, solar tech, circular economies, and biodiversity conservation.

Gamification Engine

Earn XP points for every passed quiz, level up your profile rank, and unlock achievement badges.

Verifiable Proof

Receive official printable certificates with unique security codes to showcase your environmental mastery.
